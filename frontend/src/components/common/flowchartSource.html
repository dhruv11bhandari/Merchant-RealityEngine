Case studies we referred to

Learning from systems built to spot what others miss.

We chose these case studies because each tackles a piece of the same challenge: finding misuse hidden inside legitimate-looking activity. Together, they show how to connect fragmented identities, learn normal behaviour, spot purpose changes, weigh independent evidence, and keep people in the decision loop. We use those patterns as learning references, then adapt them to our payment-focused model.

Boston Consulting Group+ QUANTEXA

Context across fragmented data

What we studied · Contextual intelligence, entity resolution, and connecting relationships across fragmented data.

What it shaped in our model

  • L1 combines data from different sources.
  • L3 identifies relationships and network patterns.
  • L4 connects independent evidence into a coherent mechanism.

Especially relevant to the Business Reality Graph and network intelligence.

Metro Bank+

Behavioural change over time

What we studied · Behavioural monitoring that spots departures from historical patterns and separates legitimate activity from suspicious change.

What it shaped in our model

  • L2 builds merchant behavioural profiles.
  • L3 uses residuals, seasonal baselines, and change-point detection.
  • L7 measures performance and refines the model.

Especially relevant to Purpose Drift Intelligence.

Legitimate infrastructure, misused

What we studied · Detecting legitimate telecommunications infrastructure being used for an unintended purpose.

What it shaped in our model

  • L3 detects unusual activity and infrastructure changes.
  • L4 identifies rotation and continuity mechanisms.
  • Cohort Continuity Engine follows suspicious behaviour even as identifiers change.

Especially relevant to account rotation and merchant replacement.

Airbnb

Declared intent versus real behaviour

What we studied · Comparing declared identity and intended use with actual behaviour, then applying proportionate interventions.

What it shaped in our model

  • L2 compares the declared business profile with actual activity.
  • L5 supports human investigation.
  • L6 guides proportionate responses and appeals.

Especially relevant to the legitimate-versus-front investigation model.

PayPal+ PALANTIR

Signals connected to evidence and investigation

What we studied · Combining machine-generated risk signals with connected evidence and human investigation.

What it shaped in our model

  • L4 fuses evidence from independent sources.
  • L5 gives analysts an investigation workbench.
  • L6 governs decisions and tracks actions.

Especially relevant to the Counterfactual Investigation Engine.
